# Saleor Storefront — add a product to the bag

A Kane CLI natural-language end-to-end test for the core storefront journey
powered by Saleor: browse the catalog and add a product to the bag. It runs in a
real browser (Kane CLI also automates mobile apps on the iOS Simulator and
Android Emulator).

## Add the first product to the bag
Go to https://storefront.saleor.io.
Wait for the product list to load.
Click on the first product card in the listing.
On the product detail page, click the "Add to cart" button.
Assert that the shopping bag shows 1 item.
